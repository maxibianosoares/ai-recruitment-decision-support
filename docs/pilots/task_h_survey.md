# Pilot Survey — AI-Powered Civil Service Recruitment Decision Support

Thank you for participating in this pilot evaluation. Your responses will
be used for research/evaluation purposes only, analyzed in aggregated and,
where possible, de-identified form. Participation is voluntary and you may
skip any question. See the attached participant information sheet
(`task_h_consent_information.md`) for full details.

**Important:** this system is a decision-support tool. The AI output is
not a final recruitment decision — the final decision always remains with
an authorized human recruitment officer. This survey asks for your honest
assessment of the AI output, including any errors you noticed.

Scale used for all rating questions:
`1 = Strongly Disagree · 2 = Disagree · 3 = Neutral · 4 = Agree · 5 = Strongly Agree`

Ready to copy into Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, or a printed
questionnaire.

---

## Section B — Usability (1-5)

B1. The system is easy to use.
B2. The information shown is easy to understand.
B3. The screening workflow (select job -> select candidate -> review -> decide)
is clear.
B4. The system is easy to learn, even without technical training.

## Section C — Usefulness (1-5)

C1. The system helps me review candidate information.
C2. The system reduces repetitive screening work.
C3. The system helps me identify relevant skills in a candidate's CV.
C4. The system helps me compare evidence across candidates.

## Section D — Explainability (1-5)

D1. The AI's recommendation is understandable.
D2. The evidence shown (requirement/evidence matrix, matched/missing
skills) helps explain the recommendation.
D3. The RAG legal/policy evidence is useful.
D4. I can identify why a specific candidate received a specific
recommendation.

## Section E — Trust (1-5)

E1. The AI's output appears consistent across candidates.
E2. I understand that the AI can make mistakes.
E3. I would verify the AI's evidence before making my final decision.

## Section F — Human oversight (1-5)

F1. The final recruitment decision should remain with authorized
recruitment officers.
F2. The AI should support, rather than replace, professional judgment.

## Section G — Open-ended questions

G1. What was the most useful part of the system?

G2. What was confusing or difficult to use?

G3. What errors (if any) did you observe in the AI's output?

G4. What additional evidence or information should the system provide?

G5. What concerns, if any, would you have about using this system in CSC
recruitment?

G6. What improvements would you recommend before any wider use?
