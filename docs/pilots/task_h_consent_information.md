# Participant Information & Consent — CSC Pilot (Task H)

**Study:** AI-Powered Civil Service Recruitment Decision Support Pilot
**Researcher:** [Your name], KOICA-Handong MS ICT Convergence Programme,
Handong Global University — in connection with your role at the Civil
Service Commission (CSC) of Timor-Leste.

## Purpose of the pilot

This pilot evaluates a prototype AI-powered recruitment **decision-support**
system for civil service recruitment. It explores whether AI-generated
candidate profiles, skill matching, rule-based screening, and
evidence-grounded (RAG) recommendations help recruitment staff review
candidates, and gathers feedback on usability, trust, explainability, and
errors — as part of academic research (paper/thesis) and to inform CSC's
own understanding of this kind of tool before any decision about further
use.

## What participation involves

You will be asked to use the system with **synthetic/dummy candidate
data** (not real applicants, unless your organization has separately
authorized a specific real-data session — see below), review one or more
candidate screenings, record your own decision and reasoning, and then
complete a short survey and/or a short interview about your experience.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is entirely voluntary. You may decline to participate, skip
any question, or stop at any time, without needing to give a reason and
without any negative consequence.

## How your feedback is used

Your responses are collected for research/evaluation purposes connected to
this pilot and the researcher's academic work. Where possible, responses
will be analyzed in **aggregated and de-identified form** (e.g. "3 of 8
participants agreed that..."). **No individual participant will be
personally evaluated, named, or singled out** in any resulting report,
paper, or thesis based on their responses.

## Data used in the pilot itself

By default, this pilot uses **synthetic/dummy candidate data only** (a
fictional job and fictional CVs already built into the system). **Your
own survey/interview responses** are the only personal data collected from
you as a participant, and are handled as described above.

If a future session of this pilot proposes to use **real candidate
personal data**, that will only happen after separate, explicit
authorization from CSC, a documented data-protection procedure, a clearly
stated and limited purpose, secure storage, and your (or the relevant
data subject's) consent. **No such authorization currently exists** — this
is explicitly marked as:

> **To be confirmed / required before any real-candidate-data collection.**

This pilot does **not** claim formal institutional ethics-board approval
exists. If CSC or the university requires such approval before formal
research data collection, that approval must be obtained first and is
tracked separately from this pilot document.

## AI system limitations (please read)

This AI system is **experimental decision support**. Its output may
contain errors, omissions, or unsupported claims. **Final recruitment
decisions remain with authorized human recruitment officers at all
times** — the AI does not, and must not, make or record a recruitment
decision on its own.
