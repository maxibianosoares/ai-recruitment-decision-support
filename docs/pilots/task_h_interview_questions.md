# Semi-Structured Interview Questions — CSC Pilot (Task H)

For recruitment officers who participated in the pilot. Intended length:
15-25 minutes. These are starting prompts, not a rigid script — follow up
naturally on anything the participant raises.

Before starting, restate briefly: participation is voluntary, responses
are for research/evaluation only, the participant may decline to answer
any question or stop at any time, and the AI system is experimental
decision support, not a replacement for their professional judgment. See
`task_h_consent_information.md`.

1. Walking through a typical candidate review with this system, what
   stood out to you compared with how you currently screen candidates?

2. Where did the AI's output (candidate profile, skill matching, rule
   screening, or RAG evidence) actually change how you thought about a
   candidate — if anywhere?

3. Was there a moment where you did not trust, or were unsure about, the
   AI's recommendation? What made you unsure?

4. How clear was it to you that the final decision was yours to make, not
   the system's? Was anything about the interface or wording confusing on
   this point?

5. If you noticed any incorrect, missing, or unsupported information in
   the AI's output, can you describe what happened?

6. How did reviewing a candidate with AI support compare, in terms of time
   and effort, with how you would normally do it?

7. What would need to be true for you to feel comfortable using a system
   like this in your actual day-to-day work at CSC?

8. What is your biggest concern, if any, about a system like this being
   used more broadly in CSC recruitment?
