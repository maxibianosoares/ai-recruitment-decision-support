# HNGV 2026 special-regime recruitment: rule catalogue

This catalogue lists the rules R01-R14 found in the ten HNGV vacancy notices of 2026 and whether each rule can be coded, partly coded, or needs human judgement.

Rule R01: Applicant is a Timor-Leste citizen, proven by valid ID card (BI), voter card and RDTL birth certificate. This rule can be coded as a deterministic rule. Gap or doubt: Authenticity of documents is still checked by a person.

Rule R02: Age at least 20 and at most 55 years. This rule can be coded as a deterministic rule. Gap or doubt: Reference date (closing date? test date?) and whether limits are inclusive are not stated.

Rule R03: Diploma at the required level and field, legalised by MESCC. This rule can be partly coded but needs a mapping list or document verification. Gap or doubt: No mapping list of fields yet; legalisation is checked from the document; CPI/4 text is inconsistent.

Rule R04: At least 3 months of work experience in the relevant field in a health facility. This rule can be partly coded but needs a mapping list or document verification. Gap or doubt: No experience letter in the document list; evidence is only the CV and certificates.

Rule R05: Mastery of Tetum and Portuguese (official languages); minimum knowledge of English. This rule needs human judgement. Gap or doubt: No threshold or test method stated; CPI/4 asks only minimum Portuguese and English.

Rule R06: Good physical and mental condition, proven by a medical certificate at the interview stage. This rule can be partly coded but needs a mapping list or document verification. Gap or doubt: Required at interview stage, not at initial submission.

Rule R07: Good conduct, proven by a valid "Bom Comportamento" certificate from the Ministry of Justice. This rule can be partly coded but needs a mapping list or document verification. Gap or doubt: Not in the 9-item submission list; when it is submitted is unclear.

Rule R08: Holds a valid professional card (MdS). This rule can be partly coded but needs a mapping list or document verification. Gap or doubt: Not in the 9-item submission list; when it is submitted is unclear.

Rule R09: Complete submission (9 items) within 7-18 September 2026 (10 working days, working hours). This rule can be coded as a deterministic rule. Gap or doubt: Period detected by OCR in 6 of 10 notices; the rest assumed the same.

Rule R10: Three sequential stages: documents, written test, interview (eliminatory). This rule can be coded as a deterministic rule. Gap or doubt: Pass rule for stage I is not described.

Rule R11: Written test score of at least 60 to continue to the interview. This rule can be coded as a deterministic rule. Gap or doubt: Whether 60 is inclusive is unclear ("valor 60").

Rule R12: Final score = simple arithmetic mean of all stage scores (0-100); suitable if >= 60. This rule can be coded as a deterministic rule. Gap or doubt: How stage I is scored is not explained; ranking against number of positions is not stated.

Rule R13: Test content weights: general 25% and specific 75%. This rule can be coded as a deterministic rule. Gap or doubt: Question content is judged by people.

Rule R14: Document assessment (stage I): criteria and how scores are given. This rule needs human judgement. Gap or doubt: Important gap for accountability and discretion; should be asked of CSC.

All ten vacancy notices (CPI/1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 13, 14, 18, 22 of 2026) use the same template. They differ in job title, category, number of positions, salary, qualification and field of experience.

## Differences between the notices and the reference statutes

Difference on pass mark boundary: the notices say score equal to or above 60 is suitable / written test "valor 60" continues; the statute says Decree-Law 34/2008 Art. 35(1): 60 points or less in an eliminatory phase = disqualified. The statute is the reference law; the notice value must be flagged for a person to decide.

Difference on minimum age: the notices say 20 to 55 years (special career regime); the statute says Law 5/2009 (amending Law 8/2004) Art. 14(b): 17 to 55 years. The statute is the reference law; the notice value must be flagged for a person to decide.

Difference on official language: the notices say mastery of Tetum and Portuguese (CPI/4: Tetum + minimum Portuguese and English); the statute says Decree-Law 34/2008 Art. 23: fluency in at least one official language. The statute is the reference law; the notice value must be flagged for a person to decide.
